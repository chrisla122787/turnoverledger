How it works

The fine print, in plain words

Privacy

Your checklists, property details, and photos are stored only in your own browser on your own device — photos in the browser's local database, never uploaded to us or anyone else. There is no account and no tracking of your properties or photos. Photos take up real space on the device, so export a backup file from the app regularly; clearing your browser data erases what's saved here.

A word about photos: the photos you take belong to your cleaning business and to the property owner — they're the owner's home, after all. Get the owner's permission before photographing a property and before sharing reports that contain photos. Keep guests' personal belongings out of frame — photograph the room and its condition, not people's things. And because your browser is the only place photos live until you export them, download each turnover's evidence pack promptly and keep it in your own storage (Drive, Dropbox, or similar) — we never hold a copy, so what's lost from the device is lost for good.

Billing & cancellation

Subscriptions are billed monthly through Stripe. You can cancel anytime from your Stripe receipt or the Stripe customer portal — no email to us required, no cancellation fee. When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and the free checklist keeps working either way.
